How to play

Tap a group of three or more matching pieces that touch side by side. Bigger groups score more, and quick chains build a streak. An inning lasts 60 seconds.

Special pieces

  • Golden Ball: joins a chain and doubles its points.
  • Stopwatch: adds five seconds.
  • Grand Slam: clears the 3×3 area around it.
  • Trophy Mug: clears every piece of one kind.

Common questions

My score isn't on the leaderboard

Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app, then play an inning. Only your best score is shown.

There's no sound

Check the music and sound switches on the start screen and the ring/silent switch on your iPhone. The game plays alongside your own music.

How do I use the discount code?

Enter it at checkout on dugoutmugs.com. The code's terms are set by Dugout Mugs.
