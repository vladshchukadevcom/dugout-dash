Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

The short version

Dugout Dash does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

Dugout Dash is made by Devcom Consulting LLC ("we") for Dugout Mugs. This policy covers the Dugout Dash app for iPhone.

What stays on your device

The app saves your best score and whether you have finished the tutorial, plus your music and sound settings. This data stays on your iPhone. We never receive it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your best score is sent to Apple's Game Center so it can appear on the "Best Inning" leaderboard. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We can see leaderboard scores and the nicknames shown there, as every player can. We do not get your name, email or Apple Account. The game works without Game Center.

The Dugout Mugs website

The reward screen can open dugoutmugs.com in Safari. The link carries a marker that says the visit came from the game. It does not identify you. What happens on that website is covered by the Dugout Mugs privacy policy, not this one.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, analytics or advertising.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the Support page.
